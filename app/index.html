⌂ RumahBudget

The Santoso household · Bandung · November 2026

RumahBudget — a household budget planner for a fictional family. All figures are realistic examples for November 2026, in Indonesian rupiah (Rp). Amounts are rounded; the ledger, the planner and the reports always agree because they share one set of numbers.

How to use it: check the overview, set your plan in the budget planner, record spending in transactions, tick off bills, watch savings goals grow, and review the half-year in reports.
